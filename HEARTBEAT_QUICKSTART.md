# ❤️ Heartbeat Quick Start Guide

Get started with Heartbeat in 5 minutes!

## Install & Run

```bash
# Navigate to the directory
cd dickerpopo

# Run Heartbeat (installs dependencies automatically)
python3 heartbeat.py

# Or make it executable and run directly
chmod +x heartbeat.py
./heartbeat.py
```

## First Time Setup

1. Enter your name when prompted
2. You'll see the main menu

That's it! You're ready to go.

## Quick Workflow

### Step 1: Add Your Important People (5 minutes)

1. Choose option **1** (Add New Relationship)
2. Start with your 3-5 closest people:
   - Name
   - Relationship type (Family, Close Friend, etc.)
   - Closeness level (1-10)
   - How often you want to connect (Weekly, Monthly, etc.)
3. Repeat for each person

### Step 2: Log Your First Interaction (2 minutes)

1. Choose option **2** (Log Interaction)
2. Select the person you recently talked to
3. Choose interaction type (Phone Call, In-Person, etc.)
4. Rate the quality (1-10)
5. Note if it was particularly meaningful

**You just earned XP!** 🎉

### Step 3: Check Connection Reminders (1 minute)

1. Choose option **5** (Connection Reminders)
2. See who needs your attention
3. Reach out to someone on the list
4. Come back and log that interaction

### Step 4: Explore Your Stats (2 minutes)

1. Choose option **6** (View Statistics)
2. See your level, XP, and progress
3. Review your connection history

## Daily Usage Pattern

**Weekly Check-in (5-10 minutes)**:
1. Open Heartbeat
2. Check reminders (option 5)
3. View all relationships (option 4)
4. Reach out to 2-3 people who need attention
5. Log those interactions afterwards

**After social events**:
- Immediately log the interaction
- Rate quality while it's fresh
- Note meaningful moments

**Acts of kindness**:
- Did something nice for someone? Log it! (option 3)
- Track your impact
- Build a kindness habit

## Understanding the Status System

When you view relationships (option 4), you'll see:
- **✅ On track** - You've connected within your goal timeframe
- **⚠️ Check in soon** - Getting close to being overdue
- **❗ Needs attention** - Past your connection goal, reach out!

## XP & Leveling System

Earn XP by:
- Adding people: 25 XP
- Logging interactions: 15 XP
- Quality time (2+ hours): 30 XP
- Acts of kindness: 20 XP
- Meaningful conversations: +35 XP bonus

Level up every 100 XP per level!

## Pro Tips for Beginners

1. **Start small**: Add 3-5 people, not 50
2. **Be realistic**: Set achievable connection frequencies
3. **Log immediately**: Don't wait - record interactions right away
4. **Use reminders**: Let the app keep you accountable
5. **Check weekly**: Make it a Sunday routine
6. **Quality matters**: Focus on meaningful connections, not just quantity

## Common Questions

**Q: How often should I use Heartbeat?**
A: Check it weekly minimum. Log interactions immediately after they happen.

**Q: Who should I add first?**
A: Your closest family and friends. Start with people you want to stay deeply connected with.

**Q: What counts as an interaction?**
A: Any meaningful connection: calls, texts, hangouts, video chats, etc.

**Q: Where is my data stored?**
A: Locally at `~/.heartbeat/data.json`. It never leaves your computer.

**Q: Can I backup my data?**
A: Yes! Just copy the `~/.heartbeat/` folder.

**Q: What if I want to start over?**
A: Delete `~/.heartbeat/` folder and restart the app.
